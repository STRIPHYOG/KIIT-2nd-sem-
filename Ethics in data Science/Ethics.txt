Data Ownership and usage:
Data belongs to the person or organization who creates or provides it.
Users should know who owns their personal data.
Data must be collected only after user permission.
Users should know why and how their data is used.
Users can access, modify, or delete their data
Users can withdraw consent anytime

Ensuring Fairness in data science:
	Data should represent all groups equally.
	Biased or incomplete data must be removed.
	Algorithms should not discriminate against anyone.
	Model results must be checked regularly.
	Human review is needed for important decisions.

Importance of ethics for data science:
	Ethics protects user privacy and personal data.
	It prevents bias and unfair decisions.
	It builds trust between users and organizations.
	It ensures responsible and legal use of data.
	It reduces harm to individuals and society.

UNIT-2 RESEARCH ETHICS

1. Data-Driven Research:

Data-driven research uses data to find answers and patterns.
Decisions are based on real data, not assumptions.
Large datasets are analyzed using statistical or ML tools.
Results are more accurate and reliable.
It is widely used in science, business, and technology.

2. Facebook Emotional Contagion Experiment:

Facebook conducted an experiment on users’ news feeds in 2014.
Users were shown more positive or negative posts without their knowledge.
The study tested whether emotions spread through social media
Results showed users’ posts changed emotionally based on what they saw.
The experiment raised serious ethical concerns about consent and privacy

3. IRB: Institutional Review Board  

Institutional Review Board is a committee that reviews research involving humans.
It ensures participants’ rights, safety, and privacy are protected.
IRB checks if proper informed consent is taken.
It prevents unethical or harmful research practices.
Research cannot start without IRB approval.

4. Methods of Data Collection
1️⃣ Surveys & Questionnaires

Data is collected by asking people questions (online/offline).

Ethics: Take consent, don’t force answers, protect privacy.

2️⃣ Interviews

One-to-one or group discussion to collect detailed data.

Ethics: Inform the person why data is taken and keep it confidential.

3️⃣ Observation

Collecting data by watching behavior or activities.

Ethics: Avoid hidden observation; respect personal boundaries.

4️⃣ Web Scraping

Data collected automatically from websites.

Ethics: Follow website rules, copyright laws, and avoid personal data misuse.

5️⃣ Social Media Data

Data taken from platforms like Facebook, Instagram, X, etc.

Ethics: Don’t misuse personal posts; anonymize user data.

6️⃣ Sensors & IoT Devices

Data from cameras, GPS, health bands, smart devices.

Ethics: Inform users, avoid continuous tracking without permission.

7️⃣ Experiments / A-B Testing

Data collected by testing changes on users.

Ethics: No harm, informed consent, approval from ethics boards.


Ethics in Qualitative Data – simple & exam-oriented 👇

1️⃣ Informed Consent

Participants must know why data is collected and agree freely.

2️⃣ Privacy & Confidentiality

Personal details should be kept secret and not shared.

3️⃣ Anonymity

Names and identities should be removed or hidden.

4️⃣ No Harm to Participants

Questions should not cause emotional, social, or mental harm.

5️⃣ Honest Data Use

Do not manipulate, exaggerate, or misrepresent responses.
